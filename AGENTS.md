Project rules and process: see `CLAUDE.md` (read `ROADMAP.md` first).

## Generating images
Use the homelab ComfyUI through `python -m tools.imagegen` (ad-hoc prompts, wallpapers) or `python -m tools.design_studio` (Nightwire character/location designs). Full how-to, presets, GPU etiquette and licence: `docs/image-generation-guide.md`. The approved look and content rules: `docs/art-direction.md`. Check the ComfyUI queue before starting a batch; never run two batches at once.
