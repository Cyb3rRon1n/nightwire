How to review (read this first)

A short is made in two rounds. You are looking at whichever round has videos below.

Round 1: grey-box (what you see now). This is a rough 3D blocking of the scene, like a storyboard that moves. The grey dolls stand in for the characters. Later, the AI video model copies the camera and the positions from this video and paints the real characters over it. So the only question is: is this the shot you want?

Judge these:

  • Camera: how close it is, from which side, and how it moves. Too far? Too close? Wrong angle?
  • Who is where: C1 and C2 (names under each title) are in the right places, and you can see both when you should.
  • Nobody blocked: a face or the important hand is not hidden behind a doll, the counter or the camera edge.
  • Timing: things happen at about the times listed under each shot (seconds into that clip), and the shot isn't too slow or too rushed.
  • Flow: watch the shots in order. Each one should follow on from the last without a jump in who sits where.

"Resting" appears twice. In the first version each doll has a faint colour so you can tell C1 from C2; in the "grey version" both dolls are plain grey. Tell Claude which one is easier to follow.

Ignore these: the grey colour, the plain dolls, missing faces and clothes, stiff movement, no sound, no lighting. The AI adds all of that.

How to send notes: tell Claude in chat. Name the shot and the second, for example "S01 at 3 s: Rin is hidden behind Saeko" or "S02: camera closer". "All fine" is a useful answer too.

Round 2: AI takes (later). Takes are the AI videos and appear next to each grey-box. Each take has buttons: Keep it, Reject it, or write what is wrong and press Needs a fix. In this round, check that the characters look like themselves, nothing changes between frames (hands, outfit, extra people), and left and right don't swap between shots.
