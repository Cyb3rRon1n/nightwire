# One connected city: classes, places and transit

Status: owner approved the direction 2026-11-06. The spec is awaiting owner review.

## The actual gap

The natural shots put characters into wallpapers made for districts, not for the characters. Some fit, others don't, because the city has no places tied to what people do or where they sleep. The night and day images also don't share a layout, and nothing shows how one district connects to the next. The owner's notes on 2026-11-05 and 11-06 kept returning to one wish: the images are beautiful, but they should feel like the same world.

This design gives the city:
- a class structure that sets how each place looks;
- a workplace and a home for each character;
- a transit network that links the districts;
- rules that keep every image recognisably Kuroshio.

It adds world text and images. It adds no game rules.

## Owner decisions (2026-11-06)

- **Old families look traditional, with their tech hidden.** Corporate money is loud. The street is the most cyberpunk.
- **Night images are the originals.** Day versions are made from them, keeping the same layout.
- **Transit:** the Ring Line and the transit network below are accepted.
- **Places:** the workplace and home table below is accepted.
- **Kaede races the Loop.** Her bio wrongly places her on the Yomi Line, which is sealed.

## Research grounding

A research pass on 2026-11-05 used fetched sources [F], search snippets only [S], or the researcher's own memory [M]. Only fetched findings count as grounding. The others are design ideas and are labelled as such.

### Class and manners
- **Ryōtei** are traditional restaurants that take guests by referral only. [F] [Ryōtei](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ry%C5%8Dtei)
- **Tea ceremony** knowledge is a long-standing marker of cultivation. [F] [Japanese tea ceremony](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_tea_ceremony)
- **Keigo** speech levels and the uchi/soto (insider/outsider) distinction set how people address each other. [F] [Keigo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Keigo)
- **Yakuza** have office and ritual customs. [F] [Yakuza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yakuza)
- **Host clubs** run tab debts. [F] [Host and hostess clubs](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Host_and_hostess_clubs)
- **Sukiya**, the restrained tea-house style, was popularised by townspeople, not only elites. [F] [Sukiya-zukuri](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sukiya-zukuri) So "the rich look traditional" is only weakly supported: it is a deliberate creative contrast, not a historical rule.
- **Cyberpunk 2077's rich** are ornate ("Neo-Kitsch"). [F] [Cyberpunk 2077](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cyberpunk_2077) Kuroshio deliberately splits that: corporate money gets the ornate glass and chrome, and old families get quiet wood with the tech behind the walls.
- **Old townhouses** (machiya) are marketed to the very wealthy. [S] [muza-chan](https://muza-chan.net/japan/index.php/blog/largest-richest-machiya-kyoto.)

### Homes
- **Danchi**, Japan's post-war public housing estates. [F] [Danchi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Danchi)
- **Capsule hotels**, where some guests rent month to month. [F] [Capsule hotel](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capsule_hotel)
- **Net café refugees**, who live in internet café booths. [F] [Net café refugee](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Net_caf%C3%A9_refugee)
- **Kamagasaki**, a day-labourer district of cheap lodging houses (doya). [F] [Kamagasaki](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kamagasaki) San'ya, a similar district. [S] [San'ya](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/San%27ya)
- **Kowloon Walled City**, a dense stacked block. [F] [Kowloon Walled City](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kowloon_Walled_City)

### Workplaces
- **Kōban**, Japan's neighbourhood police boxes. [F] [Kōban](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/K%C5%8Dban)
- **Open-plan "island" offices**, with desks in rows and the chief at the head. [S] [Noro](https://yadda.icm.edu.pl/baztech/element/bwmeta1.element.baztech-82c1c869-0eef-4045-bc01-26c3d03399fc/c/Noro.pdf)
- **Other interiors** are drawn from memory and must be checked against a source before they are cited: the yakuza office, harbour office, idol studio, clinic, shrine office, karaoke staff room, bike shop and finance office. [M]

### Transit
- **Yamanote Line**, Tokyo's loop line. [F] [Yamanote Line](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yamanote_Line)
- **Shuto Expressway**, including its C1 and C2 inner rings and the Wangan bayshore route. [F] [Shuto Expressway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shuto_Expressway)
- **Bōsōzoku** biker gangs. [F] [Bōsōzoku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B%C5%8Ds%C5%8Dzoku)
- **Monorails, shopping arcades and hillside escalators** (Chongqing, Hong Kong's Mid-Levels) are drawn from memory. [M] Tying class to transit, such as a badge needed to ride the Tenjō pods, is a design idea, not a sourced fact.

## Classes

| Class | Lives in | Look | Knows | Doesn't know | Moves by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Old families | Top of Tenjō | Wood, tatami, gardens, hidden tech; the neon city always visible below | Tea, formal keigo, who owes their family | The price of a train fare | Private pods |
| Corporate staff | Lower Tenjō towers, company housing | Glass, logos, loud chrome | Office rank, polite speech | Street codes | Sky bridges and pods, by badge |
| Working households | Danchi blocks, flats above shops in Hanabira-chō and the Docks | Worn, patched, some chrome, laundry and signage | Local favours, who owes whom | Boardroom rules | Ring Line, crowded |
| Street and indebted | Capsules, net-café booths, stacked blocks in Ikebe Low | Dense cables, status lights, hand-made neon; the most cyberpunk | Street codes, night movers, light fakers | Formal speech | On foot, by bike; a red light blacklists them from the Ring Line |

Status lights (see the debt society design) show most often in the lower two classes. Old families' chrome is either paid off or hidden.

## Transit network

- **Ring Line.** The everyday commuter loop, modelled on the Yamanote Line, with a station in every district except Tenjō. Its hub stations have shopping arcades, walkways and an underground mall. It shares signs and colours across the city. *This is new.*
- **The Loop.** The elevated ring expressway above the Ring Line, modelled on the Shuto inner rings. Ōgane drones run it by day and Kaminari Run by night, and it has arcade towers at the off-ramps.
- **Tenjō.** Sky bridges and pods above the smog line, with a badge to board. One lift station connects them down to the Ring Line.
- **Docks.** A freight line and water buses.
- **Yomi Line.** Sealed beneath everything, with a dead station under each district. No one lives there.
- **Ikebe Low.** Covered on foot: shotengai shopping streets, stairs and alleys under the expressway.

## Image rules

1. **Strong original first.** A place gets its night original approved before any day version is made. The night image must show enough wall and street detail for the outlines to carry over.
2. **Day from night: pre-light, then repaint** (found 2026-11-06 after three edge-guided recipes failed). Brighten the night original's midtones (gamma 0.75) and screen a pale cyan glow down from the top (55% outdoors, 25% indoors). Then run img2img from that picture at denoise 0.5, with the night recipe's words, "night" swapped for "clear sky, sunrise, lens flare", and the location mood kept. This keeps the original's palette, glow and rain, and gives an overcast morning with every sign still lit. Edge-guided re-rendering (lineart ControlNet on Canny edges) keeps the layout but repaints the image from noise, so it loses the original's look and comes out harsher and darker. Don't use it for day versions.
3. **Kuroshio day is barely lighter than night** (owner, 2026-11-06). Towers and structures block most of the sun, so day means a dawn-cyan sky, light only in the gaps between buildings, streets and rooms in shade, and every neon sign and window still lit. The reference is the Tenjō establishing image the owner read as sunrise. No midday sun, blue-sky brightness or flat light: those were all rejected.
4. **Tenjō windows.** Prompt for lit or reflective glass so day windows don't break into coloured specks.
5. **Original button.** The owner's "Original" choice in the World Gallery marks the base image. Otherwise a kept location image counts as a candidate.
6. **Review.** Every image is reviewed at full size before it is published. Local fixes use `tools/fix_area.py`. Content rules are unchanged: adults only, no nudity.

## Changes to the game text

Edits go in `narrator/worlds/kuroshio.toml` under its existing card rules: each card stands alone, is at most 400 characters, and has a unique name and aliases.

- **Ring Line card.** A district or transit card for the Ring Line, with the shared signs and the blacklist for red lights.
- **Primer.** A primer line on class, within the primer's size budget: old families quiet and traditional, corporate money loud, the street wired.
- **Kaede's bio.** Change "on the Yomi Line" to "on the Loop" in `narrator/worlds/designs/kaede-mori.toml`.
- **Narrator check.** Run a live narrator check that it uses the Ring Line and class manners in tone.
