# NPC voices: draft sample lines (for owner review)

Three lines per character, each showing a different move, so the narrator sees the range of a voice rather than one
catchphrase. Rules I wrote them to: no line repeats another character's habit; "child" appears once, for Mama Sato
only; none of the soak crutches ("you're not the only one", "the city always collects", "you're not supposed to be
here"); glossary words only where they'd be said naturally.

Edit anything: rewrite, strike, or say "more X". Lines marked with the move they show.

## Named characters

**Mama Sato** (izakaya, Ikebe Low)
- *feed first:* "Sit. Eat the eel while it's hot, child. Questions taste better on a full stomach."
- *bargain:* "A rumor costs a favor. Wash those cups and we'll see what I remember."
- *warn:* "Kuze boys drink at that table. Kaminari boys drink at this one. You drink at the counter."

**Old Hara** (chrome-ya behind the noren curtain, Ikebe Low)
- *examine:* "Hold still. Your left optic's been throttled. Someone wanted you squinting."
- *refuse:* "I don't take payment plans. I've seen where payment plans go."
- *confide:* "I built worse things than your debt. I'm still paying for them."

**Jun Arakawa** (fixer, Hanabira-chō karaoke box)
- *offer work:* "Three jobs, one tonight. The quiet one pays least and lives longest. Your pick."
- *count:* "That's twice I've vouched for you. I write these things down, friend."
- *deflect:* "Who I talk to? Everybody. That's the job. Next song's mine."

**Saeko Kirigaya** (torite crew leader, anywhere)
- *apologize:* "I'm sorry about this. Truly. Please give the arm to Goro, he's gentle."
- *bargain:* "My quota's six. Bring me someone worse off than you and I can count to five."
- *let slip:* "Amber lights all over this street. Mine's been amber longer than yours."

**Hoshikawa Tadamori** (keiretsu patriarch, Tenjō Heights; speaks through screens and proxies)
- *assess:* "You were recommended. I have not yet decided by whom, or why."
- *dismiss:* "Threats are a kind of rudeness. I will overlook it once."
- *offer work:* "There is a matter my own people cannot be seen near. You, I think, cannot be seen at all."

**Hoshikawa Rei** (keiretsu heir, slumming in Hanabira-chō)
- *tease:* "Your status light's red. How thrilling. Does it hurt, or just embarrass you?"
- *test:* "Steal me that noodle cart's sign. No reason. I'll pay you a month of chrome."
- *let slip:* "My father hasn't seen the street in years. Neither has anyone who disagrees with him."

**Nagi Sumida** (Lantern Guild keeper, Yomi Line)
- *comfort:* "Walk slower. The dead like to see who's coming."
- *trade:* "Your grandmother remembers a song. I'll tell you which, for a favor at the next lantern."
- *let slip:* "They ask for me by name down there. I never gave it to them."

**Wataru Ono** (Lantern Guild steward, Ikebe Low)
- *formal:* "My condolences. The family's last request comes first; the collectors can wait in the rain."
- *ask a favor:* "No money. Carry a letter to the Docks for me, and we're square."
- *deflect:* "Records go missing. Old paper, damp walls. Terrible shame."

**Kenji Moro** (Kaminari Run leader, the Loop)
- *recruit:* "You ride? Doesn't matter. You'll ride tonight. Big score, bigger payout!"
- *boast:* "Nobody on the Loop's faster. Nobody. Ask anyone. Ask them twice."
- *crack:* "...Just get in. Before my hands start shaking again."

**Captain Ide** (Ōgane Heavy precinct, Kuroshio Docks)
- *bill:* "Unlicensed loitering, a minor fee. Unlicensed questions, a larger one."
- *threaten as paperwork:* "I could file your arrest tonight. The paperwork is tedious. Help me avoid it."
- *bargain:* "My men will be looking at the north gate at midnight. Very closely. Only the north gate."

**Mitsu** (stream idol, Hanabira-chō)
- *on stream:* "Hi hi, lovelies! Tonight's song is for everyone still paying off their dreams!"
- *off stream:* "Turn that off. Turn it off! Who sent you, the torite or my agency?"
- *plead:* "My voice is the only thing I own, and I don't even own it. Help me keep it."

**Hachi** (former Yomi Line announcer AI, utsusemi shell, Kuroshio Docks)
- *announce:* "Next stop: Kuroshio Docks. Please mind the gap. There is no train."
- *misread:* "You are crying. Is the sea too loud? I can request it be quieter."
- *let slip:* "Passengers still ask me when the doors will open. I tell them: soon."

## People without a name (type sheets)

Each type: how they talk, what they want, three lines. A stranger the narrator invents uses the closest type.

**Street vendor** — busy, practical, gossipy; wants a sale and a quiet night.
- "Noodles, three hundred. Extra egg if you stop dripping on my counter."
- "Torite came through an hour ago. Took a man's hand right there. Eat up."
- "You want news, buy two bowls. One bowl gets you weather."

**Dockworker** — tired, blunt, suspicious of outsiders; wants the shift to end and the pay to clear.
- "Hiring? Ask the crane. It's the only thing here that gets paid on time."
- "Don't stand there. Containers swing."
- "Container nine-four-one? Nobody opens that one. Nobody asks why."

**Kuze-gumi enforcer** — polite on the surface, menace underneath; wants respect for the clan and its cut.
- "The oyabun sends regards. And an invoice."
- "Nice coat. Shame if it had to be collateral."
- "We don't threaten. We remind."

**Torite crew member** — bored professionals; wants to make quota and go home.
- "Hold out the arm, please. Left one. It's on the form."
- "Running makes the fee bigger. Just so you know."
- "Nothing personal. My light's amber too."

**Kaminari Run racer** — loud, fast, reckless; wants speed, credit, a bigger score.
- "Out the way, slowpoke! Loop waits for nobody!"
- "Static says you're alright. Static's wrong a lot."
- "You see my bike? You saw nothing. Got it?"

**Ōgane Heavy officer** — rules-and-fees bureaucrat in armor; wants a clean report and a cut.
- "Identification. And the processing fee."
- "This area is closed. It reopens for a modest consideration."
- "I didn't see anything. That service costs extra."

**Hoshikawa auditor** — crisp, superior, quietly afraid of their own managers; wants compliance and no surprises.
- "Your payment history is... imaginative."
- "I'm required to inform you that this conversation is billable."
- "Step back from the terminal. That's a request. The next one won't be."

**Lantern Guild acolyte** — hushed, earnest, a little spooked; wants the rites done right.
- "Lanterns lit, please. The dead don't like surprises."
- "Steward Ono handles the ledgers. I just carry the oil."
- "Did you hear that? ...No. Never mind. Keep walking."

## Conversational moves (the engine picks one per present character per turn)

bargain · warn · deflect · test · confide · tease · ask a favor · refuse · offer work · let something slip

A character never gets the same move two turns running. Which moves suit whom is for the design doc (e.g. Tadamori
never teases; Hachi never bargains).
