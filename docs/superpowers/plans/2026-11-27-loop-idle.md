# Loop Idle Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:executing-plans. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax.

**Goal:** Give her a body idle with spring follow-through, add a depth camera drift and wet light on her, all on by default and periodic.

**Architecture:**
- `idle.py` builds the body-field terms and the spring helper.
- `Motion` gains a "body" region and adds the idle and spring terms to hair and coat.
- `depth.py` makes and caches the disparity and builds the `Drift` warp.
- `wet.py` adds the light wash, sheen and drips.
- `render.py`, `checks.py` and `__main__.py` wire them in.

**Tech Stack:** numpy, OpenCV, transformers (Depth Anything V2 Small, CPU), pytest.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-27-loop-idle-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- Every layer is periodic in i with period N; grain stays the only exception.
- The face moves only rigidly. `face_max_diff` must be 0 against the still moved by the body field.
  `face_rigid` must be ≤ 0.05 px.
- Existing checks keep passing: stretch 0.9–1.1, seam ≤ 1.25, halo 0.96–1.04, repeat ≥ 1.0, ripples_on_her 0.
- The `--idle`, `--drift` and `--wet` flags are on by default, each with a `--no-` form. `--still-character` turns idle off.
- No AI-attribution trailers in commits.

## Review Focus

1. A still with no character: no idle and no wet layers, drift focused on the frame median, no crash.
2. The depth model is missing: drift is skipped with a note.
3. No hair or coat masks: idle still moves the body layer alone.
4. `--still-character` and `--no-idle`: the old path, with face_max_diff exactly 0 against the unmoved still.
5. The halo ring follows her, so a moved edge is not read as a leak.
